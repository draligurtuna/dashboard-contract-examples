A repeatable reading routine

  1. Read the field definitions. A displayed score only has the meaning assigned by its producer. A scale ending at 100 does not establish a percentage chance of success.
  2. Read the gate independently. A relative ranking and a required check can disagree without either display being broken. Write down the missing condition instead of letting an attractive number replace it.
  3. Read the observation's time basis. The time a page was opened, the time data was captured and the time a source observation closed are different facts. An older observation needs its own label.
  4. Preserve the unanswered question. Passing a discovery check does not supply an entry, a position size or an execution decision. Those require a separate process and evidence.

Three quantities, three roles

Ranking orders items under a declared rule. Qualification records whether declared checks passed. Freshness describes whether the observation meets a declared time requirement. This exercise keeps them separate rather than calculating one from another.
