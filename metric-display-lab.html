Read the boundary

A share of 0.25 becomes 25.00%. A percentage value of 25 remains 25.00%. Numeric strings are rejected, unavailable remains separate from zero, and a fractional count fails this example's safe-count rule. The negative percentage fixture is permitted; a negative share would not be.
