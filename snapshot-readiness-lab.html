What this result establishes

REVIEW_READY means this small, declared contract was met. It does not mean a financial opportunity is qualified, a position should change or a production feed is trustworthy.

The caller must establish the correct exchange/session calendar and expected completed closes. A close timestamp supplied by an untrusted payload cannot establish alignment by itself. Coverage, confirmation and declared alignment are displayed separately, even when an earlier validation gate prevents the function from continuing.

This independent example does not reproduce the proprietary Trading Suite or Smart Swap engine. No calendar inference, data-provider authentication, asynchronous atomicity, freshness policy or profitability assessment is performed.
